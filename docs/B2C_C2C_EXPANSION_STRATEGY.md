## 📊 Current State vs. Expansion Opportunity

### Current System: B2B (Business-to-Business)
```
Local Business Scraper
├─ Target: Business owners, managers, decision-makers
├─ Source: Google Maps APIs
├─ Keywords: "Plumbers", "Dentists", "Marketing Agencies"
├─ Contact: Business phone, business email, website
├─ Use Case: Connect with established business entities
└─ Market: $1.2B SMB Lead Gen Market
```

### New Market 1: B2C (Business-to-Consumer)
```
Consumer Lead Generation
├─ Target: Individual consumers interested in products/services
├─ Sources: Social media, consumer directories, review sites, classifieds
├─ Keywords: Product interest, lifestyle, demographics, behavior
├─ Contact: Personal email, phone, social profiles
├─ Use Case: E-commerce, SaaS, digital products, services
└─ Market: $4.2B Consumer Lead Gen Market
```

### New Market 2: C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer)
```
Peer-to-Peer Lead Generation
├─ Target: Individual consumers connecting with each other
├─ Sources: Marketplace listings, community groups, forums, classifieds
├─ Keywords: Services needed, interests, locations
├─ Contact: Personal phone, email, social profile
├─ Use Case: Freelancing, tutoring, services, reselling, networking
└─ Market: $2.8B P2P Marketplace Market
```

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Will this cannibalize B2B revenue?**
A: No. Different customer bases (B2B users want businesses, B2C users want consumers). Total addressable market expands.

**Q: What's the minimum viable product?**
A: Start with 1 B2C segment (ecommerce sellers) + 1 C2C segment (freelancers). This captures $2M potential.

**Q: How do we get network effects?**
A: Both B2C and C2C use automation + lead quality → more signups → better data → better leads → viral growth.

**Q: What's the unit economics?**
A: 
- B2C: $99-299/mo × 10,000 customers = $10-30M ARR
- C2C: 20% commission × $100M transaction volume = $20M ARR

**Q: Timeline to profitability?**
A: With this roadmap: Month 18-24 (after B2C + C2C launches)
